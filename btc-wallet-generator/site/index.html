Bitcoin Wallet Generator

Create a new Bitcoin wallet in your browser. Free and open source. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere.

Before you use this for real money
  1. Go offline. Download this page, disconnect from the internet, and open the file. It works fully offline.
  2. Write the recovery phrase on paper. Never photograph it, type it into a chat, or store it in the cloud.
  3. Whoever has the phrase owns the coins. Never share it with anyone, including "support" staff.
  4. A lost phrase cannot be recovered, by this site or anyone else. Tools that claim to "calculate" the private key of an address are scams.

Generates a fresh recovery phrase using your device's secure random number generator.

See the addresses of a phrase you already own, for example to check your backup. This cannot find the phrase for an address.
